# MoveProof Partner Pitch Kit

## 1-pager (what you send to partners)

### Headline
**Reduce move-out disputes with standardized evidence packs**

### One-liner
MoveProof helps tenants document condition in a consistent format and helps property teams reduce back-and-forth, saving time.

### What it does (3 bullets)
- Room-by-room capture flow (10 minutes)
- Exports a single Evidence Pack PDF + timeline (tamper-evident, LTB-friendly)
- Optional Dispute Pack workflow (demand letter + filing checklist)

### Why property teams care
- Fewer "he said/she said" arguments
- Faster turnover and less admin time
- Cleaner documentation when issues escalate

### Partner offer
- **Add to your move-in email / welcome packet**
- Rev share: 20–30% or $10 per activation
- Pilot: 30 days, no risk

## Cold call script (property manager)

> Hey — quick one. We built MoveProof to reduce move-out disputes by standardizing documentation. Tenants self-capture room-by-room photos and it generates one clean Evidence Pack PDF. That usually cuts the back-and-forth and saves your team time.
>
> If we add a link to your move-in welcome email for a 30-day pilot with rev share, who should I speak to?

**Objection: "This arms the tenant."**
> Totally get it. We position it as standardization. If there's real damage, better evidence helps you too. The goal is fewer messy disputes, not more.

**Objection: "Tenants won't use it."**
> That's why the flow is 10 minutes and the pack is one PDF. We'll also provide a 2-line blurb you can paste into your email.
